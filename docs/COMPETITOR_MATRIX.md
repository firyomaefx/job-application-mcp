## Scope conclusion

Underserved problems:

1. Verifiable claim provenance across every job-specific artifact.
2. Portable application memory across AI clients without cloud lock-in.
3. Transparent outcome learning that reports confidence and sample size.

The product will own problem 1 first. Tracking and tailoring are parity; provenance, approval, and outcome-linked memory strengthen the USP. Scraping, auto-submission, vanity dashboards, and premature hosted/payment infrastructure are distractions or risks.
