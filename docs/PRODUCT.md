# Product

## Customer and problem

Initial customer: a Malaysia-based job seeker who uses an MCP-capable AI client and wants better applications without surrendering control of career data or allowing invented claims.

The owned problem is: **turn confirmed career evidence into one reviewable, job-specific application pack in under 10 minutes, with provenance for every material claim**.

## Outcome and USP

The target outcome is an ATS-safe CV, cover letter, and checklist linked to the target job, source evidence, approvals, next action, and eventual outcome.

USP: a local-first, model-neutral career agent with evidence-locked generation, application memory, explicit approval, and bounded learning from real outcomes.

“Best” is an aspiration, not a current product claim. No controlled comparison currently proves market leadership.

## Scope

### Necessary parity

- CV/profile ingestion, job import, explainable fit, tailored documents, tracking, reminders, export, and deletion.
- English first; Bahasa Malaysia fields and copy must be fixture-tested before claiming bilingual support.

### USP strengthening

- Typed career/application graph and minimal-subgraph context assembly.
- Claim-to-evidence citations, confidence, source hashes, and user confirmation.
- Versioned pack diff, explicit approval, and outcome signals with sample size.

### Rejected or deferred

- Mass auto-apply, CAPTCHA bypass, stealth browsing, and automatic messaging.
- LinkedIn scraping/automation; LinkedIn explicitly prohibits scraping and automated activity by third-party extensions.
- Hosted sync, automated payment, and coach tenancy until isolation, PDPA, and paid-pilot gates pass.
- Graph database; relational node/edge tables are sufficient for the first measured slice.

## Golden-path acceptance

1. Confirm career evidence.
2. Import untrusted job text.
3. Classify requirements and explain fit/gaps.
4. Build a pack that blocks unsupported material claims.
5. Review a diff and approve explicitly.
6. Track the application, next action, and outcome.

Every material generated claim must cite confirmed evidence or be labelled as unverified user input.
