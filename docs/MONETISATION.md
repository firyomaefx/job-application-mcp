# Monetisation

Revenue figures are targets, not forecasts. No willingness-to-pay evidence is currently stored in this repository.

| Offer | Target price | Enforced value |
|---|---:|---|
| Community | Free | Local MCP, BYO model, profile/evidence, fit, pack, tracker |
| Hosted Pro | RM39/month | Authenticated remote MCP, encrypted sync, managed backup/recovery, advanced outcome history |
| Career Coach | RM299/month + onboarding | Consent-based multi-client workspace, isolation, workflows, reports |
| Guided Sprint | RM249 once | Setup, five verified packs, tracker, training, 30-day support |

Initial pilots use manual payment and manual server-side entitlement activation. Automated checkout is deferred.

## Validation gate

- 10 target-user interviews and 5 paid pilots.
- Measure time to first verified pack, under-10-minute activation, weekly use, approval rate, edits per claim, time saved, interview conversion, support load, and cancellation reason.
- Do not build hosted sync/payment infrastructure until paid demand and support economics are demonstrated.

## Upgrade triggers

- Hosted Pro: at least five paid users request cross-device continuity and accept the privacy model.
- Coach: at least three coaches need multi-client workflows; tenant isolation tests must pass first.
- Checkout: manual activation becomes the measured operational bottleneck.

Current entitlements, credit tools, payment webhook, accounts, and team tools are development seams and must not be sold or advertised as production services.
